Eco-Friendly Wellness Tourism Campaign

Promoting sustainable travel for a healthier planet and healthier you.

About the Campaign

Our mission is to encourage travelers to choose eco-friendly wellness destinations that support local communities, protect natural environments, and promote holistic well-being.

Survey Results

We surveyed 500 travelers in the Philippines about their eco-friendly tourism preferences.

  • 72% prefer destinations with nature-based wellness activities
  • 65% are willing to pay more for sustainable accommodations
  • 81% believe eco-friendly tourism improves personal well-being
